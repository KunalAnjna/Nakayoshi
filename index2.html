How We Work

Six steps from
brief to running

A structured process that eliminates surprises and delivers machines that work from day one.

01

Concept & Feasibility

We study your line, cycle time targets, and constraints to determine the right automation approach.

02

Design & Simulation

3D mechanical design, motion simulation, and layout planning — reviewed and signed off before fabrication begins.

03

Fabrication

In-house machining, welding, and assembly at our Pune facility using precision-grade tooling and materials.

04

Controls Integration

PLC programming, wiring, HMI development, and safety integration — all done by our controls team.

05
